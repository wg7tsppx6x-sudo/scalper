About The Rare Pull

Our story, our mission, and why we do this.

Our Story

The Rare Pull is an independent UK buyer of bulk Pokémon cards. We buy direct from collectors, players, and shops who'd rather turn a shoebox of cards into cash than spend hours listing them one by one.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make it effortless to turn unwanted bulk cards into cash, while giving every card a second life in someone else's collection or deck. We believe pricing should be transparent, quotes should be instant, and selling shouldn't feel like a chore.

What's Next

We're starting with Pokémon, but we're building The Rare Pull to grow with the hobby — expect support for more trading card games as we scale.
