Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information.

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Who we are

The Rare Pull ("we", "us", "our") buys bulk Pokémon cards from collectors, players, and shops in the UK. This policy explains what personal information we collect through therarepull.co.uk, why we collect it, and what rights you have over it.

What we collect

We collect personal information only when you give it to us directly, through:

  • The Bulk Card Calculator — if you choose to add your email address to receive a copy of your quote, along with the card quantities or weight you entered.
  • The Contact form — your name, email address, and message.

We don't require an account, and the calculator works without providing any personal information at all — the email field there is optional.

How we use it

  • To email you a quote you've requested
  • To reply to messages sent through the contact form
  • To arrange sending in and paying for cards you've agreed to sell to us

We don't use your information for marketing, and we don't sell or rent it to third parties.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of third-party services to run the site and process the information above:

  • Resend — delivers the emails the site sends (your quote, and your contact form message to us).
  • Vercel — hosts the website and its backend functions.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and data protection terms. We don't share your information with anyone else unless we're required to by law.

Cookies and local storage

We don't use tracking or advertising cookies. The Calculator page saves the quantities or weight you've entered in your browser's local storage, purely so you don't lose your place if you reload the page — this stays on your device and is never sent to us. We use privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics (aggregate page-view counts only) to understand how the site is used; this doesn't identify you individually.

How long we keep it

We keep contact form messages and quote requests for as long as needed to handle your enquiry or transaction, and a reasonable period afterwards for our own records, after which they're deleted.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct or delete it
  • Withdraw consent where we've relied on it
  • Complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) if you think we've mishandled your data

To exercise any of these, get in touch via our contact form.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
