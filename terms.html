What we do

The Rare Pull buys bulk Pokémon cards from collectors, players, and shops in the UK, either by card category or by total weight, as described on our Calculator page.

Quotes are estimates

Totals from the Calculator are estimates based on the information you provide. They aren't a binding offer. Once we receive and check your cards against your quote, we'll confirm the final amount — this may differ slightly from the estimate based on actual condition, or (for weighed collections) what's inside once we sort it.

What we accept

We currently buy mixed English-language Pokémon card collections. We don't accept:

  • Energy cards
  • Non-English cards

We reserve the right to decline all or part of a submission, for example if it doesn't match what was quoted or contains cards outside what we buy.

Sending your cards

You're responsible for packaging your cards securely and for choosing a suitable, trackable shipping method. We'll confirm sending instructions with you directly once your quote is agreed.

Payment

We pay by bank transfer. Once your cards arrive and we've checked them against your quote, payment goes out within a few business days.
