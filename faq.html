Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about selling your bulk cards.

Use our Bulk Card Calculator to get an instant quote, either by counting your cards into categories or just weighing the whole lot. From there, send us a message through the contact form to arrange sending your cards in. Once we receive and check them against your quote, we pay out by bank transfer.

Bulk covers mixed English-language Pokémon collections across the categories on our Calculator page, from commons right up to gold/rainbow/secret rares. We don't currently buy energy cards or non-English cards. If you're not sure whether your collection qualifies, send us a message and we'll take a look.

We use flat per-card rates based on condition and rarity — commons and uncommons, holos and reverse holos, V/EX/GX, VMAX/VSTAR, illustration rares, and gold/rainbow/secret rares each have their own rate. You can see our current rates on the Calculator page at any time.
